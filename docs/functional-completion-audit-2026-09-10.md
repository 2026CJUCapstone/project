## Reading the matrix

`U` = unit/static/mock; `L` = isolated ASGI/DB integration; `E` = actual multi-service/browser/image integration; `M` = actual SMTP delivery and inbox/mail-sink confirmation; `P` = approved production deploy/failure/rollback evidence.

Status is deliberately split into bounded completion and gaps:

- **Bounded complete** means the named functions and result artifact demonstrate the listed scenarios at the stated level. It does not cover every input, race, device, browser, or release.
- **Partial / weak** means useful normal or negative evidence exists, but a required level, current-release binding, screen, or boundary is missing.
- **Missing / blocked** means there is no qualifying evidence, or an external prerequisite is unavailable.
